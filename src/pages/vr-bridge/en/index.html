nyan Real / VR Bridge

VR Bridge

Windows software for using supported glasses as a SteamVR headset. It displays VR images for each eye and moves the view as you rotate your head.

Currently in development

A free test release is being prepared. Public downloads, setup instructions, terms of use and support channels will be listed here when available. Purchasing Spatial Wall is not required.

Target environment

  • 64-bit Windows and SteamVR.
  • One supported pair of glasses connected directly to the PC, configured as an extended display.
  • Standard firmware and Full-SBS at 3840×1080 / 60 Hz.

Development targets are XREAL Air / Air 2 / Air 2 Pro / One / One Pro / 1S and xbx a01+. Per-model compatibility and verification status will be listed with the test release. ROG XREAL R1 and xbx a01 are outside the current scope.

Current scope and limitations

  • Seated 3DoF use with head rotation tracking. Position tracking and a pair of VR hand controllers are outside the current scope.
  • Compatibility with all VR titles, extended use and wearing comfort have not been verified.
  • If the glasses disconnect during use, exit SteamVR normally and restart it.
